Read [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md), then [START.md](../../../START.md). Resume from the local `.agency-commons/` state if present; verify recorded access and deployment claims against actual tools and files.

Perform the technical work. Ask the person for business facts, needed preferences, account/consent actions, and concrete external decisions. Keep the initial additional-spend budget at $0. Teach small useful ideas as they arise; don't require coding lessons before a safe launch.

Use [the session guide](../../../coach/SESSION.md) for short explanations, observable learning evidence, and resuming a conversation. The working artifact and the person's understanding are separate outcomes.

Read only the relevant runbook:

- Operating system or shell differences: [computer setup](../../../docs/COMPUTERS.md).
- Competitors, positioning, copy and page structure: [discovery](../../../docs/DISCOVERY.md) and [research-and-position](../research-and-position/SKILL.md).
- Design, components and responsive review: [frontend](../../../docs/FRONTEND.md) and [frontend-quality](../frontend-quality/SKILL.md).
- First preview, dependency installation, Cloudflare connection or publication: [setup](../../../docs/SETUP.md).
- Agent tool selection and authentication: [tools](../../../docs/TOOLS.md).
- Domain/DNS or business inbox work: [domains and email](../../../docs/DOMAINS-AND-EMAIL.md).
- Receiving enquiries: [forms](../../../docs/FORMS.md).
- Search visibility, analytics, tags or campaigns: [growth](../../../docs/GROWTH.md).
- Blog planning, drafting, publication or updates: [blog management](../../../docs/BLOG.md).
- Recurring reports, content drafts or client email drafts: [background agents](../../../docs/AUTOMATIONS.md).
- Account permissions, secrets, data and recovery: [security](../../../docs/SECURITY.md).
- Delivering to a client: [handoff](../../../docs/HANDOFF.md).

Use one project copy per client and the client's intended accounts. Preserve existing working services. Prefer a connected MCP/CLI/API operation to manual dashboard navigation, then verify the result. Never claim a connection, publication, form delivery or campaign change succeeded without evidence.

Do not create a hosted agency platform, shared multi-tenant application, ecommerce system, or payment integration as an implied part of a marketing-site task. Follow the person's explicit scope and budget.
