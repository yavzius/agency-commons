## Start with the problem you want to solve

Describe what currently makes the room difficult to use. A room that feels crowded needs a different conversation from one that needs better light.

## Bring useful constraints

Note the furniture you want to keep, how you use the room, and the budget you are comfortable discussing. Photos and rough measurements can help explain the situation.

## Agree on the next step

Ask what the designer needs before suggesting an approach. The business owner should verify any description of their process before this article is published.
