# Work

Use stable task IDs. Each task has an owner, next action, and evidence before completion. States: backlog, ready, in-progress, waiting-client, review, done.

| ID | Task | State | Owner | Due date | Next action / blocker | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T001 | Confirm the brief and service scope | ready | unassigned | unset | Gather the essential business facts | none |

Keep recurring obligations here: domain renewals, form checks, content reviews and reporting. Dates need an agreed time zone. A due date does not activate a background schedule.
